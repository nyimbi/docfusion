## Finding Organizations by Capacity Level

### Large International Organizations (Annual Budget $10M+)

**Filter in Excel:**
- Annual_Budget_Range = "$10M - $50M" OR "Over $10M" OR "$5M - $10M"
- Organization_Type contains "International"

**Examples:**
- ICRC (International Committee of Red Cross)
- MSF (Médecins Sans Frontières)
- CARE International
- Catholic Relief Services
- IOM (International Organization for Migration)
- UNHCR, WFP, UNICEF (UN agencies)

### Medium-Sized Organizations ($1M - $10M)

**Ideal for:**
- Targeted program grants
- Consortia partnerships
- Capacity building initiatives

**Filter in Excel:**
- Annual_Budget_Range = "$1M - $5M" OR "$5M - $10M"

**Examples:**
- WANEP national chapters
- CDD-Ghana
- KAIPTC (Kofi Annan International Peacekeeping Training Centre)
- National human rights organizations

### Grassroots & Emerging Organizations (Under $1M)

**Ideal for:**
- Innovation grants
- Community-level interventions
- Pilot programs

**Filter in Excel:**
- Annual_Budget_Range = "$50K - $250K" OR "$250K - $1M"
- Organization_Type = "Local NGO" OR "Community-Based Organization"

**Examples:**
- Local peace committees (Sahel)
- Women's peacebuilding networks
- Youth-led organizations
- Faith-based community groups

## Priority Classifications Explained

### CRITICAL PRIORITY (47 organizations)
**When to use:** Emergency grants, conflict prevention, active conflict zones
**Characteristics:**
- Operating in active conflict areas (Sudan, Libya, Sahel, DRC, CAR)
- Specialized peacebuilding or early warning capacity
- Demonstrated track record in crisis response

**Examples:**
- WANEP (early warning across West Africa)
- UNSMIL (Libya peace process)
- Carter Center Sudan (mediation)
- IOM Sahel (displacement and peacebuilding)

### HIGH PRIORITY (164 organizations)
**When to use:** Major humanitarian grants, established partnerships, multi-year programs
**Characteristics:**
- Established organizations with proven capacity
- Multi-sector programming
- Strong financial management
- Regional or international presence

**Examples:**
- ICRC, MSF, CARE International
- National Red Cross/Red Crescent societies
- Major UN agencies (UNHCR, WFP, UNICEF)

### MEDIUM-HIGH PRIORITY (54 organizations)
**When to use:** Sector-specific grants, innovation funding, capacity building
**Characteristics:**
- Specialized thematic expertise
- Emerging organizations with strong potential
- Niche sectors (climate security, digital rights, specific populations)

**Examples:**
- KAIPTC (peacekeeping training)
- CDD-Ghana (governance and CVE)
- Specialized research institutes

### MEDIUM PRIORITY (90 organizations)
**When to use:** Coordination support, network grants, indirect programming
**Characteristics:**
- Coordination mechanisms
- Support organizations
- Membership networks
- Capacity building providers

## Common Search Scenarios

### Scenario 1: "I need peacebuilding partners in Sudan"

**Steps:**
1. Open "Peacebuilding" sheet
2. Filter Country_of_Operation = "Sudan"
3. Sort by Priority_Classification (CRITICAL first)
4. Review 11 organizations
5. Check Unique_Value_Proposition and Current_Programs
6. Contact top 3-5 matches

**Expected Results:** Carter Center, International Alert, Conciliation Resources, UNDP Sudan, IOM Sudan

---

### Scenario 2: "I want to fund local women's organizations in conflict zones"

**Steps:**
1. Open "Women & Gender" sheet
2. Filter Organization_Type = "Local NGO"
3. Filter Country_of_Operation for conflict countries (Sudan, Libya, DRC, CAR, Mali, Somalia)
4. Review 15-20 matches
5. Check Annual_Budget_Range for appropriate capacity level

## Data Verification Tips

### High Confidence Organizations (Data Score 80+)
- Complete contact information verified
- Website accessible and current
- Multiple source verification
- Recent activity confirmed (2024-2025)
- **Recommendation:** Safe to contact directly

### Medium Confidence Organizations (Data Score 60-79)
- Most contact information available
- Website may need verification
- 2-3 sources consulted
- **Recommendation:** Verify website before contact

### Lower Confidence Organizations (Data Score <60)
- Limited public information
- Contact details may be outdated
- Fewer verification sources
- **Recommendation:** Cross-check with regional networks before contact

## Excel Power User Tips

### Useful Formulas

**Count organizations by country:**
```
=COUNTIF(Country_of_Operation, "Sudan")
```

**Find organizations by budget range:**
```
=FILTER(Master_Database, Annual_Budget_Range="$1M - $5M")
```

**List all organizations with email contacts:**
```
=FILTER(Master_Database, Contact_Email<>"Unknown")
```

### Advanced Filtering

**Multi-criteria filter:**
1. Select Master Database sheet
2. Data > Filter > Advanced Filter
3. Criteria examples:
   - Region = "West Africa" AND Priority = "CRITICAL"
   - Focus_Areas contains "Peacebuilding" AND Budget < $1M
   - Organization_Type = "Local NGO" AND Data_Score > 70

### Creating Custom Views

**Save frequently used filters:**
1. Apply your filter criteria
2. View > Custom Views > Add
3. Name your view (e.g., "Sudan Peacebuilding Partners")
4. Quick access via View > Custom Views
