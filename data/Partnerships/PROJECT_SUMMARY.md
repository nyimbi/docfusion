## Executive Summary

This comprehensive database contains **439 verified NGOs** operating across Africa, compiled from multiple authoritative sources including international databases, regional networks, government registries, and grant-maker records. The database prioritizes organizations working in peacebuilding, conflict prevention, early warning systems, and humanitarian response.

## Quality Assurance & Verification

### Data Verification Process
- Cross-referenced across minimum of 2-3 sources
- Website accessibility verified where provided
- Operational status confirmed (2024-2025 activity)
- Duplicate organizations identified and consolidated
- International NGOs with multiple offices consolidated into single entries

### Limitations & Considerations
1. **Language Bias:** Research primarily conducted in English; French, Arabic, and Portuguese-speaking organizations may be underrepresented
2. **Internet Access Bias:** Organizations without websites or online presence are underrepresented
3. **Conflict Zone Gaps:** Active conflict areas (DRC, CAR, Sudan, Somalia) have limited accessible information
4. **Small Organization Coverage:** Grassroots organizations, women's groups, and community associations need further research
5. **Contact Information:** Many organizations have limited public contact details due to privacy and security considerations
