SECTION 1: BACKGROUND AND OBJECTIVES

The Metropolitan Government serves over 1.2 million residents across 45 municipal divisions and operates with an annual IT budget of $85 million. Our current technology infrastructure, implemented between 2015-2018, is reaching end-of-life and requires comprehensive modernization to support digital government initiatives, enhance citizen services, and ensure cybersecurity compliance with evolving federal mandates.

The primary objective of this procurement is to engage a qualified vendor to design, implement, and maintain a next-generation digital government platform that will serve as the foundation for all citizen-facing services, internal operations, and inter-governmental data sharing for the next 15 years.

SECTION 2: TECHNICAL REQUIREMENTS

2.1 SYSTEM ARCHITECTURE AND PERFORMANCE

2.1.1 Core Infrastructure Requirements
The proposed solution MUST implement a cloud-native, microservices-based architecture capable of supporting a minimum of 500,000 concurrent users during peak periods (tax filing season, election registration). The system SHALL achieve 99.99% uptime with no single point of failure, utilizing active-active redundancy across geographically distributed data centers within 100 miles of the metropolitan area.

All services MUST be containerized using Kubernetes orchestration with automatic scaling capabilities that can handle 10x traffic spikes within 30 seconds. The solution SHALL support hybrid cloud deployment with seamless failover between on-premises and cloud resources.

2.1.2 Performance Benchmarks
Response time for citizen portal pages MUST NOT exceed 1.5 seconds under normal load conditions and 3 seconds during peak usage. Database query performance SHALL maintain sub-200ms response times for 99% of transactions. File upload and download speeds MUST support 50MB files with completion times under 30 seconds on standard broadband connections.

The system SHALL process a minimum of 100,000 transactions per hour during business hours and support batch processing of 10 million records overnight without impacting daytime performance.

2.1.3 Scalability and Capacity Planning
Initial deployment MUST support 2.5 million registered users with capability to scale to 5 million users within 18 months. Storage requirements include 500TB of active data with 2PB archival capacity, growing at 20% annually. The system SHALL automatically provision additional resources when utilization exceeds 70% and de-provision when below 30% for cost optimization.

2.2 SECURITY AND COMPLIANCE FRAMEWORK

2.2.1 Cybersecurity Standards
All components MUST comply with NIST Cybersecurity Framework 2.0, FedRAMP High baseline controls, and CJIS Security Policy requirements. The solution SHALL implement zero-trust architecture with continuous authentication and authorization for all users and services.

Encryption MUST utilize AES-256 for data at rest and TLS 1.3 for data in transit. All cryptographic implementations SHALL be FIPS 140-2 Level 3 validated. The system MUST support hardware security modules (HSMs) for key management and digital signing operations.

2.2.2 Identity and Access Management
Multi-factor authentication (MFA) is MANDATORY for all user accounts, with support for FIDO2, SMS, email, and biometric authentication methods. The system SHALL integrate with existing Active Directory infrastructure and support SAML 2.0, OAuth 2.1, and OpenID Connect protocols.

Role-based access control (RBAC) MUST provide granular permissions management with support for dynamic policy enforcement based on user context, device trust level, and network location. Privileged access management (PAM) controls are REQUIRED for all administrative functions.

2.2.3 Audit and Monitoring
Comprehensive logging MUST capture all user activities, system events, and security incidents with tamper-evident storage for minimum 10 years. Real-time security monitoring SHALL detect and alert on anomalous behavior within 60 seconds using AI/ML-based threat detection.

Security Information and Event Management (SIEM) integration is REQUIRED with existing IBM QRadar deployment. The solution MUST generate automated incident response workflows and provide digital forensics capabilities for security investigations.

2.3 INTEGRATION AND INTEROPERABILITY

2.3.1 Legacy System Integration
The solution MUST seamlessly integrate with 47 existing legacy systems including:
- SAP ERP system (version 6.0 EHP8) for financial management
- Oracle Database 19c clusters (12 instances, 150TB total)
- IBM FileNet P8 document management system
- Cityworks asset management platform
- ESRI ArcGIS Enterprise for geographic information systems
- Microsoft Exchange Server 2019 for email and collaboration
- Cisco Unified Communications Manager for voice services

API integration SHALL utilize RESTful web services with OpenAPI 3.0 specifications. Legacy mainframe connectivity MUST support CICS transactions and DB2 database access through secure middleware layers.

2.3.2 Data Integration and ETL
Real-time data synchronization is REQUIRED between all integrated systems with event-driven architecture supporting 1 million events per hour. Extract, Transform, Load (ETL) processes SHALL handle incremental and full data migrations with data quality validation and error handling.

Master Data Management (MDM) capabilities MUST ensure data consistency across all systems with support for data lineage tracking, version control, and audit trails. Data integration SHALL support both batch and streaming processing patterns.

2.4 USER INTERFACE AND ACCESSIBILITY

2.4.1 Citizen Portal Requirements
The citizen-facing web portal MUST provide responsive design supporting desktop, tablet, and mobile devices with consistent user experience across all form factors. The interface SHALL comply with WCAG 2.2 AA accessibility standards and Section 508 requirements.

Multi-language support is REQUIRED for English, Spanish, French, Arabic, and Chinese (Simplified) with right-to-left text support. The portal MUST integrate with translation services for additional language support based on demographic analysis.

2.4.2 Administrative Interfaces
Staff and administrative interfaces SHALL provide role-based dashboards with customizable widgets and reporting capabilities. The system MUST support single sign-on (SSO) integration with existing Windows domain authentication.

Advanced search and filtering capabilities are REQUIRED across all data types with full-text indexing and natural language query support. Batch operations MUST support processing of thousands of records with progress tracking and error reporting.

SECTION 3: FUNCTIONAL REQUIREMENTS

3.1 CITIZEN SERVICES MODULE

3.1.1 Online Service Applications
The system MUST provide digital forms for all 127 currently offered government services including permits, licenses, registrations, and benefit applications. Each form SHALL include intelligent field validation, auto-completion, and document upload capabilities supporting PDF, image, and office document formats up to 25MB per file.

Application status tracking MUST provide real-time updates with automated email/SMS notifications at each workflow stage. Citizens SHALL have ability to save partial applications and resume completion within 30 days.

3.1.2 Payment Processing
Integrated payment gateway MUST support credit cards, debit cards, ACH transfers, and digital wallets (Apple Pay, Google Pay, PayPal) with PCI DSS Level 1 compliance. The system SHALL handle payment plans, partial payments, and automated recurring billing for subscriptions and installments.

Fee calculation engine MUST support complex tiered pricing, discounts, penalties, and tax calculations based on configurable business rules. Payment reconciliation SHALL provide automated matching with accounting systems and exception handling for failed transactions.

3.1.3 Document Management
Citizens MUST have secure access to personal document vault with unlimited storage for official documents, certificates, and correspondence. Document generation SHALL produce PDF outputs with digital signatures and blockchain-based authenticity verification.

Version control and document history are REQUIRED with audit trails for all access and modifications. Bulk document operations MUST support batch downloads and sharing with designated third parties through secure links.

3.2 INTERNAL OPERATIONS MODULE

3.2.1 Workflow Management
Configurable workflow engine MUST support parallel and sequential processing with conditional branching based on business rules. The system SHALL provide visual workflow designer for non-technical staff with drag-and-drop interface.

Task assignment and routing MUST consider staff workload, expertise, and availability with automated escalation for overdue items. Performance metrics and SLA monitoring are REQUIRED for all business processes with management dashboards.

3.2.2 Case Management
Comprehensive case management MUST link related applications, permits, and violations with unified citizen profiles. The system SHALL support case notes, attachments, and communication history with full audit trails.

Integration with scheduling systems is REQUIRED for inspections, hearings, and appointments with automated calendar synchronization and reminder notifications. Mobile capabilities MUST support field staff with offline data access and synchronization.

3.2.3 Reporting and Analytics
Ad-hoc report generation MUST provide drag-and-drop interface with 500+ pre-built templates covering all operational areas. The system SHALL support scheduled report distribution via email, FTP, and web services.

Real-time analytics dashboards are REQUIRED for executive leadership with drill-down capabilities and comparative analysis. Data visualization MUST include charts, maps, and interactive elements with export capabilities to multiple formats.

SECTION 4: DATA REQUIREMENTS

4.1 DATA MIGRATION AND CONVERSION

4.1.1 Legacy Data Migration
Complete migration of historical data from 47 legacy systems is REQUIRED with zero data loss tolerance. The migration SHALL include 25 years of historical records totaling approximately 850TB of structured and unstructured data.

Data cleansing and normalization processes MUST identify and resolve duplicate records, missing values, and format inconsistencies. The vendor SHALL provide data quality assessment reports with recommendations for ongoing data governance.

4.1.2 Migration Timeline and Validation
Data migration MUST occur in phases with parallel system operation during transition period not exceeding 6 months. Each phase SHALL include comprehensive validation testing with user acceptance criteria.

Production cutover MUST be completed during scheduled maintenance windows with rollback capabilities in case of critical issues. Post-migration data integrity verification is REQUIRED within 30 days of each phase completion.

4.2 ONGOING DATA MANAGEMENT

4.2.1 Backup and Recovery
Automated backup procedures MUST create full system snapshots every 4 hours with incremental backups every 15 minutes. Backup retention SHALL maintain daily backups for 90 days, weekly for 1 year, and monthly for 10 years.

Disaster recovery capabilities MUST achieve Recovery Time Objective (RTO) of 2 hours and Recovery Point Objective (RPO) of 15 minutes. Annual disaster recovery testing is REQUIRED with documented procedures and performance metrics.

4.2.2 Data Archiving and Retention
Intelligent data archiving MUST automatically move inactive records to low-cost storage based on configurable retention policies. The system SHALL maintain instant access to archived data through seamless user interface integration.

Legal hold capabilities are REQUIRED for litigation and regulatory compliance with immutable storage options. Data destruction MUST follow certified procedures with completion certificates for regulatory compliance.

SECTION 5: IMPLEMENTATION REQUIREMENTS

5.1 PROJECT TIMELINE AND MILESTONES

5.1.1 Implementation Schedule
Total implementation timeline MUST NOT exceed 24 months from contract execution. The project SHALL be divided into six 4-month phases with defined deliverables and acceptance criteria for each phase.

Phase 1: Infrastructure setup and security implementation (Months 1-4)
Phase 2: Core platform deployment and legacy system integration (Months 5-8)  
Phase 3: Citizen portal development and testing (Months 9-12)
Phase 4: Internal operations modules and workflows (Months 13-16)
Phase 5: Data migration and user acceptance testing (Months 17-20)
Phase 6: Production deployment and optimization (Months 21-24)

5.1.2 Critical Dependencies
The vendor MUST coordinate with existing maintenance windows for legacy systems and comply with government fiscal year budget cycles. Implementation SHALL minimize disruption to daily operations with no service interruptions exceeding 4 hours during business hours.

Change management and user training MUST begin in Phase 2 with comprehensive training programs for 2,400 government employees across 45 departments.

5.2 TESTING AND QUALITY ASSURANCE

5.2.1 Testing Requirements
Comprehensive testing MUST include unit testing, integration testing, system testing, performance testing, security testing, and user acceptance testing. Test coverage SHALL achieve minimum 95% code coverage for all custom developed components.

Load testing MUST simulate peak usage scenarios with 500,000+ concurrent users and validate all performance requirements. Security testing SHALL include penetration testing by certified ethical hackers with detailed vulnerability assessments.

5.2.2 Quality Metrics
Defect density MUST NOT exceed 2 defects per function point for delivered software. All critical and high-severity defects SHALL be resolved before production deployment. The system MUST pass independent security assessment by approved third-party auditors.

User acceptance testing MUST achieve 95% satisfaction rating from representative user groups across all departments and citizen demographics.

SECTION 6: SUPPORT AND MAINTENANCE

6.1 ONGOING SUPPORT SERVICES

6.1.1 Technical Support
24/7/365 technical support MUST be provided with maximum 15-minute response time for critical issues and 4-hour response time for non-critical issues. On-site support SHALL be available within 2 hours for hardware-related problems.

Support team MUST include certified professionals with government sector experience and clearance requirements. Escalation procedures SHALL provide direct access to senior engineers and solution architects for complex issues.

6.1.2 Maintenance and Updates
Regular system maintenance MUST occur during approved maintenance windows with advance notification to all stakeholders. Security patches SHALL be applied within 72 hours of vendor release with emergency patching capabilities for zero-day vulnerabilities.

System updates and enhancements MUST maintain backward compatibility with existing integrations and customizations. Version control and rollback procedures are REQUIRED for all system changes.

6.2 SERVICE LEVEL AGREEMENTS

6.2.1 Availability Requirements
System availability MUST maintain 99.99% uptime measured monthly with penalties for non-compliance. Planned maintenance SHALL NOT exceed 8 hours per month and MUST occur during approved maintenance windows.

Unplanned downtime MUST NOT exceed 30 minutes per incident with automatic failover capabilities to backup systems. Service level credits SHALL apply for availability failures based on contractual penalty structures.

6.2.2 Performance Guarantees  
Response time guarantees MUST be maintained under all normal operating conditions with continuous monitoring and alerting. Performance degradation exceeding 50% of baseline SHALL trigger automatic scaling and vendor notification.

Capacity planning reviews are REQUIRED quarterly with recommendations for infrastructure adjustments based on usage trends and growth projections.

SECTION 7: VENDOR QUALIFICATIONS

7.1 MINIMUM QUALIFICATIONS

The vendor MUST demonstrate:
- Minimum 10 years experience in large-scale government IT implementations
- Successful completion of at least 5 similar projects with budgets exceeding $50 million
- Current employees with active security clearances appropriate for government work
- ISO 9001, ISO 27001, and CMMI Level 3 or higher certifications
- Financial stability with bonding capacity for projects of this magnitude

7.2 PROPOSAL REQUIREMENTS

Proposals MUST include:
- Detailed technical architecture diagrams and specifications
- Complete project timeline with critical path analysis
- Staff resumes and organizational chart showing dedicated team members
- Client references from similar government implementations within past 5 years
- Total cost of ownership analysis for 10-year operational period

SECTION 8: EVALUATION CRITERIA

Proposals will be evaluated using the following weighted criteria:
- Technical Approach and Solution Architecture (35%)
- Implementation Timeline and Project Management (20%)
- Vendor Experience and Qualifications (20%)
- Total Cost of Ownership (15%)
- References and Past Performance (10%)

Performance guarantees: Required with penalty clauses for non-compliance
