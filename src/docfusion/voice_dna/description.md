# Voice DNA Package

## Purpose
Organizational voice profiling and style validation system that creates "voice fingerprints" and ensures all content maintains authentic organizational tone and style consistency.

## Key Responsibilities
- **Voice Profiling**: Creating multi-dimensional voice profiles from historical documents
- **Style Validation**: Real-time validation of content against organizational voice patterns
- **Authenticity Scoring**: Measuring content alignment with established voice profiles
- **Rhetorical Analysis**: Recognizing and validating persuasion patterns and authority signaling
- **Voice Evolution**: Tracking and maintaining voice profiles over time

## Service Dependencies
- **NLP Package**: Consumes style analysis and text processing services for voice profiling
- **Storage Package**: Stores and retrieves voice profiles and historical document corpus

## Service Providers
- **AI Agents**: Provides voice consistency validation for content strategy
- **Document Engine**: Supplies style validation and authenticity scoring services
- **All Content Creators**: Offers voice compliance validation across DocuFusion

## Architecture Role
Style authority in the AI & Intelligence Layer that ensures organizational voice authenticity without generating content - focuses solely on validation and profiling.
