# Compliance Package

## Purpose
Automated compliance management system that ensures all documents meet regulatory requirements, submission standards, and organizational policies through intelligent validation and risk management.

## Key Responsibilities
- **Regulatory Compliance**: Validation against government and industry regulations
- **Submission Standards**: Ensuring format, structure, and content compliance with RFP requirements
- **Risk Management**: Identifying and mitigating compliance risks and gaps
- **Evidence Management**: Organizing and validating compliance documentation
- **Audit Support**: Maintaining comprehensive audit trails and compliance reporting

## Service Dependencies
- **NLP Package**: Consumes regulatory analysis and compliance requirement extraction services
- **Storage Package**: Accesses compliance databases and regulatory document repositories
- **Discovery Package**: Receives regulatory context and compliance requirements from opportunities

## Service Providers
- **Document Engine**: Provides compliance validation and format standards enforcement
- **AI Agents**: Supplies compliance strategy and validation workflows
- **Workflow Package**: Offers compliance checkpoints and approval processes

## Architecture Role
Compliance authority in the Document & Content Layer that ensures all DocuFusion outputs meet legal, regulatory, and submission requirements while minimizing organizational risk.
