# Discovery Package

## Purpose
Proactive opportunity hunting and RFP analysis system that continuously monitors global funding sources, intelligently filters opportunities, and provides comprehensive competitive analysis for strategic advantage.

## Key Responsibilities
- **Comprehensive RFP/EoI Scraping**: Automated monitoring of 200+ sources including:
  - Government: SAM.gov, Grants.gov, DOD, NASA, NIH, NSF, state/local portals
  - Commercial: BidSync, GovWin, RFPDB, Ariba, JAGGAER, Coupa platforms
  - Foundations: Foundation Center, Candid, private foundation portals
  - International: TED Europa, World Bank, UN, country-specific procurement
- **Real-Time Monitoring**: Continuous scanning with change detection and alerts
- **Content Analysis Coordination**: Managing requirement extraction via NLP services
- **Intelligent Filtering**: AI-powered relevance matching and capability alignment
- **Competitive Intelligence**: Tracking competitor activity and bidding patterns

## Service Dependencies
- **NLP Package**: Consumes requirement extraction and document analysis services
- **Storage Package**: Uses search services for opportunity discovery and historical analysis
- **Intelligence Package**: Receives competitive landscape and win probability assessments

## Service Providers
- **AI Agents**: Provides structured opportunity data and competitive intelligence
- **Intelligence Package**: Supplies market data and competitive activity information
- **Compliance Package**: Offers regulatory context and eligibility requirements

## Architecture Role
Market intelligence hub in the Document & Content Layer that transforms reactive proposal processes into proactive strategic opportunity capture through continuous discovery and analysis.
