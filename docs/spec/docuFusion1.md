### ⚙️ Key Capabilities  

#### 1. **Intelligent Document Composition Engine**  
- **Generative AI Drafting**:  
  - Auto-generates text, tables, and technical diagrams from bullet points .  
  - Multimodal output (PDF/DOCX/HTML) with dynamic cross-references.  
- **Agentic Workflows**:  
  - AI agents research RFP requirements, draft responses, and validate compliance .  
  - Autonomous web scraping for opportunity discovery and preemptive response drafting.  

#### 2. **End-to-End RFP Lifecycle Management**  
- **Ingestion & Analysis**:  
  - Parses RFP documents to extract scoring criteria, deadlines, and mandatory sections.  
  - Auto-generates compliance matrices with gap heatmaps .  
- **Predictive Scoring**:  
  - Benchmarks drafts against historical winners using machine learning.  
  - Flags weaknesses (e.g., missing case studies) with improvement prompts.  

#### 3. **Enterprise-Grade Collaboration**  
- **Contextual E-Signatures**:  
  - Version-locked signing workflows with blockchain audit trails .  
  - Compliance with eIDAS/ESIGN/UETA standards.  
- **Role-Specific Workspaces**:  
  - Parallel editing for technical, legal, and financial teams with section locking.  

#### 4. **Competitive Intelligence Hub**  
- **Corporate Memory System**:  
  - Auto-harvests winning content into reusable snippets tagged by AI .  
  - Tracks competitor messaging and pricing strategies via integrated web scraping.  

#### 5. **Seamless Ecosystem Integration**  
- **CRM Connectivity**:  
  - Bidirectional sync with Salesforce/Dynamics for real-time data binding .  
  - Automated annexure generation from CRM records (e.g., case studies, resumes).  
- **Payment Orchestration**:  
  - Milestone-triggered invoicing via 30+ gateways (Stripe, Adyen).
